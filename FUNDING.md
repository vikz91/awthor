# Support Awthor

Awthor is a free, open-source, local-first writing app. It is built to give writers a calm place
to work while keeping manuscripts on their own device by default.

Financial sponsorship is not open yet. We are preparing a GitHub Sponsors profile with both
one-time and monthly options. When it is available, the repository's **Sponsor** button and this
page will point to the official funding channel.

In the meantime, starring the repository, sharing Awthor with a writer, reporting a reproducible
bug, improving the documentation, or contributing a focused pull request all help the project.

## What sponsorship will sustain

Sponsorship will support the work required to keep a privacy-conscious writing tool dependable:

- hosting, domains, and the infrastructure behind optional sync and publishing;
- security updates, dependency maintenance, browser compatibility, and release work;
- accessibility, performance, documentation, issue triage, and contributor support; and
- focused development of features that serve Awthor's local-first writing experience.

The first priority will be recurring project costs and maintenance. If funding grows beyond those
needs, it can fund larger improvements and paid work on well-scoped community contributions.

## Funding principles

- **Awthor stays free and open source.** Sponsorship will not put the core writing experience
  behind a paid tier or change the project's AGPL-3.0 license.
- **Writers' privacy is not for sale.** Sponsors receive no manuscript data, usage data, account
  access, or influence over Awthor's privacy and security boundaries.
- **Sponsorship does not buy the roadmap.** Ideas are considered on their value to writers,
  alignment with the project's scope, maintenance cost, and community feedback.
- **Recognition is optional.** Public sponsors may be thanked in project materials with their
  consent. Private sponsorships remain private.
- **Support is not a commercial contract.** Sponsorship does not include guaranteed response
  times, private support, endorsements, or custom development.

## Transparency

Once Awthor begins receiving sponsorships, this page will identify the active funding channels.
When the amounts are meaningful enough to allocate, we will also publish periodic summaries of
what the funding supported. Individual sponsors and private financial details will not be
disclosed without permission.

## Organizations

If your organization uses, evaluates, or learns from Awthor, sponsorship is a direct way to help
maintain the public project. Please open a
[GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/vikz91/awthor/discussions) before proposing recognition,
funded work, or another arrangement. Security reports must remain private and follow the
[Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

Thank you for helping make private, portable writing tools available to everyone.
